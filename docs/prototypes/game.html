Recommended base

True Tabletop

The phone behaves like a physical table between two players. Each player owns a separate tray on their side, and the action instruction rotates toward the active player.

Placement Both trays are visible. Tapping a board point automatically moves one identical stone from the active tray.
After placement The empty trays collapse. Player strips become compact and the board receives more visual attention.
Shared-device clarity The top player’s name, count and stones are rotated 180° so they read naturally from the opposite side.
Information Only the current instruction stays visible. Stats and controls open in one compact sheet.
Best overall choice. It gives Shaxda a physical identity without sacrificing board size or beginner clarity.

Maximum board

Board Max

Player areas are reduced to thin edges, allowing the board to occupy the largest possible square on short or narrow phones.

Strength The board is the dominant object and touch targets can be larger.
Trade-off Player identity and the physical-basket feeling are weaker than in True Tabletop.
Best use A useful compact mode for very small screens or experienced players.
Information A floating “i” button avoids reserving any permanent sheet handle space.
Best secondary mode. Keep this as a future “focus board” option rather than the default first experience.

Strong turn ownership

Active Console

The active player’s side expands into a clear control area while the waiting player’s side compresses. This makes turn-taking unmistakable on one shared phone.

Strength Players immediately know who should touch the board next.
Trade-off The layout visibly changes every turn, which may feel busy during fast play.
Placement Only the active player’s full basket is emphasized; the other basket compresses.
Movement Expanded areas can show selected-stone guidance without using the right-side dashboard.
Good alternative, not my first choice. It is very clear, but the constant expansion and collapse may distract from the board.
Recommended product direction

Start from Concept 1. Keep two separate top-and-bottom trays only during placement, collapse them for removal and movement, rotate the instruction toward the active player, and keep secondary information inside the compact sheet. Sponsor placement remains a later design decision and does not consume active-play space.
